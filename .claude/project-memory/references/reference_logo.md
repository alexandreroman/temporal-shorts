# Official Temporal logo

`src/assets/temporal-logo-horizontal-light-cropped.svg` is the official white
horizontal Temporal lockup (symbol + wordmark), published with the brand
assets at https://temporal.io/brand. The only change is the viewBox, cropped
from `0 0 2400 1200` to `405 395 1570 410` to remove the margin.

**Why:** the video is published under the Temporal brand; a redrawn or
approximated logo is off-brand.

**How to access:** take the light horizontal lockup from the Temporal brand
assets and re-apply the viewBox crop if the file must be refreshed. The logo
appears above the intro title, large at the start of chapter 7, and on the
end card.
