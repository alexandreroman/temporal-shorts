# Camera moves within a scene

In themes with camera shots (a scene's `makeCamera` layer framed by
`setCamera`), the camera travels one way within a scene: a progression
along the composition, or one push-in and one pull-back at most. It never
goes left, right, then left again. When the story needs something from
the other side, the content comes to the camera (an element slides,
grows or docks into the current shot) rather than the camera going back.
A single move back is fine when it is itself a story beat (a rewind).

**Why:** back-and-forth moves read as redundant and tiring; each camera
move must carry the story forward.

**How to apply:** when planning a scene's shots, list its framings in
order with their times and check the direction of each move; a reviewer
of a scene with camera moves checks that list.
