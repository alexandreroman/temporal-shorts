# Recap chapter: What you get

Every theme ends with a recap chapter titled **What you get**: the last
numbered chapter, in its own scene file, right before the un-numbered outro.

- `durable-execution`: chapter 9, `09-benefits.js`
- `agent-harness`: chapter 8, `08-recap.js`
- `durable-ai-agents`: chapter 8, `08-recap.js` (budget payoff and 4 tiles)
- `human-in-the-loop`: chapter 6, `06-recap.js` (benefit tiles, then the
  use cases the pattern fits)

The recap is a chapter of its own, separate from the last topic chapter;
the recap is not a topic, so topic lists in `docs/<theme>/script.md` leave
it out.

**Why:** a recap appended to a topic chapter makes that chapter's title
contradict part of its content; a dedicated chapter keeps every title true
to what is on screen.

**How to apply:** a new theme gets a "What you get" chapter before its
outro; keep recap tiles and subtitles there, never in the topic chapters.
